# The Lion and the Little Mouse

**Premise:** A tiny mouse spares a sleeping lion's pride, and later the small one frees the big one from a hunter's net.

**Source:** Aesop, "The Lion and the Mouse" (public domain). Total running time: 60 seconds.

## Scene 1 (7 s)
- **Shot:** Wide, slow push in across golden savanna grass to a lion asleep under an acacia tree.
- **We see:** Warm late afternoon light. A small grey mouse scurries through the grass.
- **Narration:** "On a warm, sleepy afternoon, a great lion was napping under a tree."
- **Dialogue:** none
- **Sound:** Soft breeze, crickets, a gentle snore.

## Scene 2 (6 s)
- **Shot:** Low close-up at mouse height, camera follows the mouse.
- **We see:** The mouse runs straight up the lion's tail and across his back, then freezes on his nose.
- **Narration:** "A little mouse, in a hurry, ran right over his nose."
- **Dialogue:** none
- **Sound:** Tiny pitter-patter steps, a big snort.

## Scene 3 (8 s)
- **Shot:** Close-up on the lion's face, eyes open; quick cut to a top-down shot of a huge paw landing beside the mouse, pinning its tail.
- **We see:** The lion wakes, grumpy. The mouse is caught by the tail, trembling.
- **Narration:** "Snap! The lion woke up, and the mouse was caught."
- **Dialogue:** Lion (rumbling): "Who dares wake me?"
- **Sound:** Low growl, a soft thump.

## Scene 4 (8 s)
- **Shot:** Two-shot, eye level with the mouse, the lion's face filling the background.
- **We see:** The mouse clasps its tiny paws, looking up bravely.
- **Narration:** none
- **Dialogue:** Mouse (small, quick voice): "Please let me go! One day, I could help you."
- **Sound:** Quiet, plucked strings.

## Scene 5 (7 s)
- **Shot:** Medium close-up on the lion, slight tilt as he laughs, then lifts his paw.
- **We see:** The lion chuckles, lifts his paw, and the mouse scampers away, waving.
- **Narration:** "The lion laughed. How could something so small help someone so big? But he let the mouse go."
- **Dialogue:** none
- **Sound:** Deep warm laugh, light flute.

## Scene 6 (8 s)
- **Shot:** Wide, static, dusk colours; then a quick zoom in on the lion.
- **We see:** Days later, the lion walks into a rope net hidden in the grass. The net lifts and wraps around him.
- **Narration:** "A few days later, the lion stepped into a hunter's net."
- **Dialogue:** Lion: "Help! Help!"
- **Sound:** Rope creak, a roar that echoes.

## Scene 7 (8 s)
- **Shot:** Low tracking shot following the mouse running toward the net; then close-up of tiny teeth on a rope.
- **We see:** The mouse hears the roar, races over, and starts nibbling the thick rope, knot by knot.
- **Narration:** "The little mouse heard him. She ran as fast as she could, and she nibbled, and nibbled."
- **Dialogue:** none
- **Sound:** Quick nibbling, rising hopeful music.

## Scene 8 (8 s)
- **Shot:** Medium shot, net falls open; slow pull back to a wide shot of the two together under the acacia at sunset.
- **We see:** The lion steps free. He lowers his head; the mouse sits on his paw. They watch the sunset side by side.
- **Narration:** "The rope fell away. And the lion knew that even the smallest friend can be a great help."
- **Dialogue:** Lion (softly): "Thank you, little friend."
- **Sound:** Music resolves, gentle evening birds.

**Total: 7 + 6 + 8 + 8 + 7 + 8 + 8 + 8 = 60 seconds.** Every scene is one shot of 8 seconds or shorter (scenes 3, 7 and 8 use a cut or camera move inside one 8 second clip).

## Character sheet
**Lion**
- Look: big, soft golden mane, round friendly face, sleepy eyes, storybook watercolour style.
- Voice: deep, slow and rumbling; grumpy at first, then warm.
- Arc: proud and amused, ends humble and grateful.

**Mouse**
- Look: small grey mouse, large round ears, pink nose, a bright brave look.
- Voice: high, quick and earnest, never squeaky to the point of silly.
- Arc: scared but brave, keeps her promise.

**Narrator**
- Look: never seen.
- Voice: calm, kind grandparent voice, steady pace for ages 4 to 8.
- Role: tells the story and lets the pictures show the moral.
